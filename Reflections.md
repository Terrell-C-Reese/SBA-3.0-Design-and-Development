Challenges you encountered during the project.


As previously stated in the README documentation, the biggest issue I ran into was utilizing the gradient feature. Once i found 
the proper documentation and supplementary videos to assist in my understanding of implementation, I was able to complete this
portion of the project. Additionally, I'd like to mention that breaking down the images in the .zip file helped me come up with
a solid plan to break down what I saw into modules.


Your approach to solving these challenges.


My approach to solving this challenge was to simply break down the images into modules and focus on building from the bottom up. My 
prior experience working with other languages combined with my understanding of OOP helped guide me through the challenge in a way
that felt organized and concise.


Improvements you would make if given more time.


To be honest, with more time I think I could have used less CSS than I currently have in the project. I'm sure there could have been 
better ways to organize how I wrote my HTML. Perhaps, there were easier ways to implement the structuring of the cards or maybe something
built into bootstrap or tailwind that could've assisted with styling that I overlooked or just didn't know existed.
